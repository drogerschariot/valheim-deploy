Valheim Deploy

Run your own Valheim servers on your Windows PC, from one dashboard.

Download for Windows

Free. Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. No account needed.

Valheim Deploy is for Windows PCs only for now.

This is an advanced setup. The server runs on your PC, so for friends outside your home to join you'll need to sign in to your router and forward ports, allow the game through Windows Firewall, and know your public IP address. The app walks you through all of it and does what it can for you, but some internet providers (those using CGNAT) make home hosting impossible. If that sounds like a lot, a paid Valheim host will be a much better experience.

Early release. Expect rough edges, and keep backups of worlds you care about.

How it works

  1. Run ValheimDeploy-Setup.exe. The app isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may say “Windows protected your PC”: choose More info, then Run anyway. It installs for you only, with no admin prompt.
  2. Double-click Valheim Deploy on your desktop. The dashboard opens.
  3. Create a server. The app downloads the Valheim server from Steam (about 1 GB, once) and starts it.
  4. Work through the Network tab: firewall, router port forwarding, and a check of your internet connection. Then send friends the join address and password.

What you get

  1. As many servers as you like, all in one dashboard, each started with one click or automatically when you open the app.
  2. Live status, players, logs, daily backups, and one-click game updates.
  3. Closing the window keeps your servers running from the system tray. Quit stops them, saving every world first.
  4. Your PC, your servers, your worlds. Nothing runs through us.
