Valheim Deploy

Your own Valheim server, set up from your browser.

Download

Free. No account needed to download.

Windows · Mac (Apple Silicon) · Linux

This is an advanced setup. You run the server yourself, so you should be comfortable with firewalls, public IP addresses, port forwarding and SSH. Cloud hosting bills you until you delete the server. If that sounds like a lot, a paid Valheim host will be a much better experience.

Early release. Expect rough edges, and check your hosting account after deleting a server.

How it works

  1. Download the app and open it. There is nothing to install.
  2. The app is not code-signed yet, so the first time you open it your computer warns you:
    • Windows: on “Windows protected your PC”, choose More info, then Run anyway.
    • Mac: open the downloaded zip, then double-click valheim-deploy-macos-arm64. When macOS says it was “Not Opened”, choose Done. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, choose Open Anyway, and confirm. After that it opens normally.
  3. Your browser opens. Choose where to host: Hetzner Cloud, AWS, a Linux server you already have, or your own computer.
  4. Follow the steps, then share the join address and password with your friends.

What you get

  1. As many servers as you like, all in one dashboard.
  2. Live status, players, logs, daily backups and game updates.
  3. Your hosting account, your servers and your data. Nothing runs through us.
