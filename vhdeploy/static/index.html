    Friends outside your home reach this server through your router. Work down this list: each
       item says what's wrong and, where it can, fixes it for you.
    
    
    Checking your network…
    
    
    
      
      
    
    
    
    
      What do these words mean?
      
    
        
    Public IP address
    
        
    The one address the internet sees for your whole home. Friends join with it. Your internet
            provider can change it, often when the router restarts.
    
        
    LAN IP address
    
        
    This PC's address inside your home, such as 192.168.1.50. Only devices on your own network can
            use it. Your router hands these out and can change them, which is why a reservation helps.
    
        
    Port forwarding
    
        
    A rule in your router that says "game traffic arriving on these ports goes to this PC". Without
            it the router doesn't know where the traffic belongs, and drops it.
    
        
    UPnP
    
        
    A way for apps to ask the router for a port forward themselves. Many routers have it; some
            ship with it off.
    
        
    CGNAT
    
        
    When your internet provider shares one public IP between many customers. Nothing you set at
            home can then let friends in. Ask the provider for your own public IPv4 address.
    
        
    Firewall
    
        
    Windows' own filter for incoming traffic. It needs a rule allowing the game in.
